Two engines, one chart

Attach it to an XAUUSD chart and it starts looking for trades straight away. Both engines run at the same time and use separate magic numbers, so you can tell their trades apart.

Engine 1 · magic 777001

Scalping

  • M1, M5 and M15, all sessions, no daily limits
  • Nine strategies, 4 positions per signal
  • Targets at 1R, 2R, 3R and 5R, one structural stop
  • Break-even after +1R, ATR trail after +2R
Engine 2 · magic 777002

News

  • Predicts the release and enters one second before it
  • Up to 8 positions, sized from free margin
  • Adds when the actual agrees, flips when it does not
  • Closes whatever is left after 30 minutes

Install in five steps

  1. Copy the files

    In MT5 choose File, Open Data Folder and copy the MQL5 folder from the zip over the one there. The picture goes to MQL5\Images\MIB_bg.bmp.

  2. Compile

    Open ManInBlackEA.mq5 in MetaEditor and press F7. It should finish with no errors.

  3. Allow the news API

    Go to Tools, Options, Expert Advisors, tick Allow WebRequest for listed URL and add https://financialmodelingprep.com.

  4. Add your key

    Paste your FinancialModelingPrep key into InpFmpKey. Without a key the robot falls back to the MT5 built-in calendar.

  5. Attach and switch on

    Open an XAUUSD chart, drag the EA onto it and turn Algo Trading on. You need a hedging account.

The nine scalping strategies

Each one works on closed bars and only proposes a direction and a structural stop. The engine then checks spread and stop size, sizes the lots and opens the group. The tag appears in the trade comment, for example MIB SB M5 2/4.

SB

ICT Silver Bullet

03-04, 10-11 and 14-15 New York time. Liquidity sweep, structure shift, then the first retest of a fresh fair value gap.

NYO

New York open

09:45 to 11:30 NY. Judas swing of the 09:30-09:45 range, or a breakout with displacement. Once per side per day.

LS

Liquidity sweep

An untouched swing high or low is wicked through and rejected, with a wick of at least half the bar.

OB

Order block sniper

The last opposite candle before a displacement of 1.2 ATR or more. Only the first tap counts.

FVG

FVG sniper

An untouched gap of at least 0.25 ATR, first retest only. An EMA trend filter is optional.

SD

Supply and demand

A tight base of up to three small candles, then a departure of 1.3 ATR or more. Trades the first return.

BR

Break and retest

A swing level broken by a strong close, then a first retest that holds and closes back out.

MOM

Momentum

A body of at least 1 ATR closing at its extreme, volume 1.2 times average, with the EMA trend.

PA

Pure price action

Pin bar or engulfing candle at an EMA pullback or a 20-bar extreme.

Guard rails keep it aggressive without being careless: spread limits, stops between 0.6 and 3.0 ATR, no entry if price has already passed the stop, a margin-level check, and caps of 12 scalp positions per direction and 24 in total. There is no daily loss or profit limit.

What the news engine does

For each release at or above your impact level (High by default) in your currencies (USD by default).

  1. T minus 60 s

    Refresh the numbers

    Forecast and previous values are pulled again from the API.

  2. T minus 1 s

    Predict and enter

    The robot scores the release. If confidence is at least 1.0 it opens up to 8 positions at once, each with its own target on a 1R to 10R ladder.

  3. T to T plus 90 s

    Wait for the actual number

    The API is polled every 1.5 seconds until the real figure arrives.

    Actual agreesMore positions are added in the same direction.
    Actual contradictsThe news positions are closed and the trade is flipped.
    No prediction was madeIt trades the actual number instead.
  4. T plus 30 min

    Time stop

    Any news position still open is closed.

What the prediction uses

Whether the last release of that indicator beat or missed its forecast, whether the forecast improves on the previous number, and whether gold is already trending into the release. The reasons are printed in the Experts tab after Based on:.

Direction

Stronger US data means sell gold, weaker means buy gold. Unemployment and jobless claims are treated as inverse, so a higher number is bad for the dollar.

Read this before going live

Full margin can empty an account

You asked for full margin on news. The default uses 90% of free margin but caps the total loss at 30% of balance if every stop is hit. Setting InpNewsMaxLossPct to 100 removes that cap.

A prediction is an estimate

A pre-release entry is a forecast, not a certainty. Gold spreads and slippage at NFP or CPI can be very large, so entries are skipped above InpNewsMaxSpread.

Check the news clock once

At UTC+2 the NFP line should read 15:30 in winter and 14:30 during US summer time. If it does not, adjust InpNewsTimeFixHours and check your PC clock.

Test first

Run it on a demo account and in the Strategy Tester. Points assume 2-digit gold, where one point is $0.01. Free FMP plans have a daily request limit, which the default refresh rate is tuned for. Trading carries risk and nothing here guarantees profit.
